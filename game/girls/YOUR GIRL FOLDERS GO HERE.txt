### How to install girl packs for Brothel King ###

* Download some girl packs online (see https://henthighschool.net/brothel-king/ for information)

* Unzip the girlpacks into the 'game/girls/' folder

* Each girlpack has its own folder (should be called after the girl's name)


### Advanced tips ###

* You can change or add more 'girls' folders in BKsettings.rpy (but all such folders must be situated within the 'game' folder or subfolders)

* You can create girl mixes automatically by adding a 'mix' folder. A mix folder must start with '#'.
Example:
girls/
	#onepiece/
		Nami/
		Boa Hancock/
This will create a mix (#onepiece) and add two girlpacks to the mix automatically (Nami and Boa Hancock)

* You can ignore folders within the 'girls' folder by adding underscore '_' at the beginning of the folder name.
Example:
girls/
	Nami/
		_story/
The pictures in '_story' will be ignored and excluded from the girl pack

* All subfolders contained within a girlpack will be used for the girlpack unless prefaced with '_'
Example:
girls/
	Nami/
		profile/
The pictures in 'profile' will be added to the 'Nami' girlpack.

* You are not allowed to have two girlpacks of the same name at the same time. If you have two girl packs of the same name, either rename one to something different or add '_' in front of one of the folders to ignore it.

* _BK.ini files allow some interesting customization for advanced girlpack makers (see https://henthighschool.net/brothel-king/ for information on girl customization)
